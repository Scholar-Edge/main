How billing works

  • Checkout and payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your plan switches on within a minute of payment.
  • You can update your card, download invoices or cancel anytime from the Account page. Cancelling stops the next payment, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Your results and progress stay on your account whichever plan you are on.
