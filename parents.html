For parents

Registration, practice materials, special arrangements, results and looking after your child.

A summary of what parents usually need to do. Each school can add its own rules, so contact the school with any question.

Before you register

  • Check each school's website to confirm your child is eligible and that a scholarship is offered at their year level.
  • Find out if the school wants extra documents, and send them by the due date.
  • If two schools test on the same alternative date, you can only pick one.
  • Check closing dates. Late applications are not accepted.
  • Each school sets its own fee, and each needs a separate application.

Applying

  1. Create a parent account and application on the test provider's scholarship portal.
  2. Enter your details and your child's.
  3. Choose the school and level.
  4. Upload anything the school asks for.
  5. Pay the registration fee.

Practice materials

The test provider sells online and downloadable practice tests through the parent account, for Primary and Levels 1 to 3. There are no past papers. Sit them like the real test to learn about timing.

Special arrangements

For injuries or disabilities, you need medical evidence less than 12 months old, and applications go in at least 4 weeks before the test. Results are not adjusted afterwards as special consideration.

Living far away

If you live more than 200 km from a participating school, you can request another centre or a different test centre, at least 4 weeks ahead. A surcharge applies: $200 within Australia and $250 overseas.

Results and offers

In cooperative sittings, you are emailed when results are available in your parent account. In alternative sittings each school decides whether and how to report. Schools contact you directly about scholarship offers.

Looking after your child

Make sure they sleep well, avoid piling on pressure, stay relaxed yourself, and keep expectations realistic. Not finishing is normal for these exams.
