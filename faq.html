Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions parents ask most.

Should my child prepare at all?

The test providers say short-term training is unlikely to help and that wide reading and critical thinking are the best preparation, though they offer practice tests for familiarity. Steady practice that focuses on understanding mistakes is a sensible middle path.

How long should we prepare?

Advice ranges from about 8 weeks to a year or more, and none of it is backed by solid evidence. Start early, practise steadily and review mistakes.

Can anyone guarantee a scholarship?

No. Outcomes depend on how strong the student is to start with, effort, test conditions and how competitive each school is. Be wary of anyone who promises a place.

Do I have to sit the test more than once?

Not if all your schools are in the same cooperative sitting. Schools on different dates each use their own test, so your child sits each one.

Do wrong answers lose marks?

For ACER scholarship tests, no. Check the rules of your own test.

Is the Victorian selective exam the same as a scholarship test?

No. It is a state-wide ACER exam for Year 9 entry to four government schools, with its own format. Scholarships are run school by school.

How long should I practise each day?

Short, regular sessions beat long cramming. There is no official figure, and anyone quoting a precise number of hours is guessing.

Can ScholarEdge mark writing?

No. It gives prompts, a planning step and a checklist. A teacher or tutor needs to read the piece.

Where are my results stored, and who can see them?

Your profile and results are stored with your account in an online database. Each student can read only their own results, and the tutor or administrator who runs the platform can review them. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and ScholarEdge never sees or stores card details.

Can I delete my results?

Yes. On the Progress page, choose Reset my results to delete your saved results. To close your account completely, contact the site owner.

How many questions are there?

The numbers change as questions are added, so they are listed on the What’s inside page, including where the question bank is thinner.

Does ScholarEdge explain the answers?

Not yet. After a test you can review every question with the correct answer and your own answer, and the home page sample shows what a worked explanation looks like. Written explanations for the full question bank are not available yet.

What does the free plan include?

Practice sessions, one trial exam, one writing task and every guide. See Pricing for the full comparison.

Are the questions from real papers?

No. They are original practice questions modelled on common formats. ScholarEdge is not affiliated with ACER or Edutest.
