Exam day tips

What to do on the paper, with the clock, and on the day.

Practical advice for the paper, the clock and the day itself. Always follow the instructions for your actual test.

On the paper

  • Read the front-cover instructions of every paper.
  • If answers are shaded on a machine-scanned sheet, check often that each answer sits against the right question number.
  • Expect it to feel hard. Many students leave questions blank, and not finishing is common.

Guessing and time

  • For ACER scholarship tests, marks are not deducted for wrong answers. If that applies to your test, a blank is a guaranteed zero and a guess is not.
  • If you can rule out two options, choose the likelier of the other two and move on. Come back if time allows.
  • Do not sink time into one question. Know how long each section is and track your progress at intervals.

Writing

  • Plan for a couple of minutes, then write to the prompt.
  • Show original thinking and organise your ideas clearly.

The day itself

  • Sleep well and arrive at least 15 minutes early. Latecomers, if admitted, get no extra time.
  • Bring two HB or B pencils, an eraser and a sharpener, a blue or black pen for the writing, a bottle of water and a healthy snack for the break.
  • Leave behind rulers, books and papers, calculators, watches, phones, cameras, and maths instruments such as compasses.
