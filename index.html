What is inside

Sit it like the real thing

A full exam screen with a countdown clock, a question navigator, flagging and a review page before you submit. Sit one subject, or a full paper with a headed section for each subject.

No sugar-coating

Every answer is saved by topic. If you get fractions right a third of the time, you are told so, and told what that means at scholarship level. Targeted quizzes then aim at those topics.

Read before you spend money

Plain guides to how ACER and Edutest tests work, the Victorian selective exam, preparing, test-day rules and what parents need to check.

How it works

  1. Make a profile. Your results are saved to your account, so they follow you to any device.
  2. Choose an exam, a year level and a mode. Subjects change with the year you pick.
  3. Open Progress for your accuracy by topic and an honest assessment.
  4. Run a Targeted quiz on the weak topics, then repeat until the numbers move.

What this is not

The questions are our own, modelled on common formats, not official ACER or Edutest papers. The site cannot mark writing, so Written Expression practice needs a teacher to read it. The verbal and reading question banks are small, so expect repeats.
