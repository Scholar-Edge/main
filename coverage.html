How the questions are made and checked

Every question is written for ScholarEdge. None is copied from an official paper, and the picture-based formats are represented as tables and text. Numerical and logic question types are generated, and automated checks recalculate the answers independently on tens of thousands of generated questions. Passages and fixed items are written by hand. The site remembers which questions you have already seen on your device, and avoids them where it can.

Where the limits are

  • Writing is not marked by the site.
  • After a test you see every question, the correct answer and your own answer, but written explanations are not available yet.
  • There are 12 long reading passages, enough for about two full Humanities sections. After that the app fills the gap with shorter items rather than repeat a passage, and says so in your report.
  • The question history is kept on one device, so using another device can show repeats.
  • Questions are original practice material, not official papers, and no result is guaranteed.
