Sources

Where the information came from, and which sources are selling something.

Pages used to write the guides, checked on 13 October 2026. Start with the official ACER pages.

  • ACER Scholarships: parent information. Official. Sittings, levels, registration, practice materials, test day, special arrangements.
  • ACER Victorian Selective Entry: Prepare. Official. Exam format, strategies, tips for parents.
  • ACER Scholarship Tests exam tips sheet (a PDF supplied to us). Official.
  • ACER Victorian selective entry sample questions (PDFs supplied to us, © ACER). Used only to see the style of question. No question on this site is copied from them.
  • ACER Scholarship Tests: Example Questions, 2017 (a PDF supplied to us, © ACER). Official. Used for format details only. None of its questions are reused.
  • A 2022 selective entry practice set (reading, verbal, numerical, mathematics and answer grid, labelled Melbourne High, plus creative and persuasive writing tasks carrying Victorian Department of Education branding). Supplied to us. Used only to see the format and style. Nothing from it is copied.
  • Selectivetrial: preparing for ACER scholarship exams. A commercial practice-test seller.
  • Exam Success: ACER Year 7 Level 1 guide. A commercial tutoring and course seller. Its test-length figures are estimates.
  • Lynn's Learning: selective and scholarship prep in Victoria. A commercial tutoring centre.
  • EduCourse: ACER scholarship test guide. A commercial course seller. Several details differ from ACER (see the Test guide).
  • Strive for Excellence: scholarship preparation. A tutoring business. Used only for preparation timing and the point that no one can guarantee a place.
  • Learnmate: scholarship and selective tutors. A tutor marketplace. Its description of ACER's sections does not match ACER's own materials.
  • A Reddit thread on entrance exams (r/vce). We could not open it, so nothing here draws on it.
The commercial sites sell products. We have used them only for general advice and flagged their claims. ScholarPrep is not affiliated with any of them, or with ACER or Edutest.
